Getting there

It says San Francisco, but it’s Burlingame, fifteen miles south of the city and four miles from SFO.

From the airport

The venue

Hyatt Regency San Francisco Airport, 1333 Old Bayshore Hwy, Burlingame. The hotel and the ballrooms are the same building.

Free shuttle

It runs 24 hours, about every 15 to 20 minutes. From terminals 1 to 3, grab your bags and go up to Departures/Ticketing. Follow the Hotel Courtesy Shuttle signs to the center island. From International, stay on Arrivals and follow the same signs.

The van says Hyatt Regency / Marriott. If you can’t find it, call the hotel at (650) 347-1234.

Uber or Lyft

Put in Hyatt Regency San Francisco Airport, 1333 Bayshore Highway, Burlingame. From terminals 1 to 3 the pickup is Level 5 of the Domestic Garage. From International, go up to Departures/Ticketing (Level 3) and out to the center island, zones 12 to 17. The app shows the exact spot.

Once you’re there

Check-in

Congress check-in opens Thursday Nov 19 at 9 PM. Bring a photo ID and your ticket, printed or on your phone.

Performer pass

It covers every salsa, bachata, kizomba and zouk class. It also gets you into the Friday, Saturday and Sunday shows and the afternoon and night parties. Bootcamps and master classes aren’t included.

Rehearsal

Tech rehearsal is 2 to 6 PM every day in Grand Ballroom D. You need the performer pass to get in.

Tickets

Online sales close Sunday Nov 15. After that it’s their WhatsApp, 415-794-2007.

Still need a room?

The congress block at the Hyatt was sold out for Friday as of Nov 10. Call the hotel and ask if the SF SBK group rate is open for your other nights. These are close on foot:
